# Response governance — proposed gates
Advisory-only by default. Deterministic authorization separate from AI. Model judges advisory, not sufficient for release approval. CI must reject failed invariant tests; use only bounded synthetic fixtures and mock services. Evaluate pinned Promptfoo/PyRIT/garak versions and licenses before adoption; no live targets or autonomous offensive generation.
For later containment: exact authorized scope, dry run, expiration, reversible change, rate/impact budgets, protected admin and recovery channels, circuit breaker, audit trail, human approval bound to exact structural changes. Test legitimate traffic spikes, missing signals, stale approvals, rollback failure and resource limits. No hack-back or auto privilege escalation. No automatic system changes in alpha.
Regulatory applicability review remains open: EU AI Act 2024/1689 (classification and relevant oversight/recordkeeping), NIS2 2022/2555 (entity scope and national implementation), GDPR (minimization, lawful basis, retention), CRA 2024/2847 (product scope, vulnerability handling, reporting and transition). Confirm current official text and applicable obligations before distribution; no certification or legal conclusion claimed. Record policy version, reason, confidence, approval and outcome without retaining secrets.
